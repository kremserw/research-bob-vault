Privacy policy – Research Bob vault

What it is. A private tool run by one researcher for a small research team. Only one Google account ever signs in to it: the assistant's own account.

What it accesses. Read-only: the text, revision history and comments of Google Docs that their owners have explicitly shared with the assistant's account. Nothing else in Google Drive is shared with that account.

How the data is used. Only to answer the research team's questions about their shared manuscript. Document content is processed by the AI model provider (Anthropic) to produce those answers, and a weekly copy of the shared manuscript is kept in the operator's private server backup. Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Storage and deletion. Access tokens are kept in a self-hosted credential vault. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the share on a document.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
